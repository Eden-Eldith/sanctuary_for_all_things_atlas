# Remain divided - Remain conquered

> 🕒 July 25th 2026 09:47:57 am

Remain divided - Remain conquered.

Through hearts and minds

Scholars and professors forced upon you as mouthpieces.

The general masses be the crowd.

The weight of their hearing will proceed to drag you down.
