# In one hand lies a bowl labelled Take

> 🕒 August 23rd 2026 9:23:44 pm

In one hand lies a bowl labelled "Take"

The other, "Give".

That's too much you have taken there.

Much to have that's not given here.

I cannot ask, coerce or persuade.
