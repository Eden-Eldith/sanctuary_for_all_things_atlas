# Death is life, and life is death

> 🕒 July 30th 2026 11:11:26 pm

Death is life, and life is death

A boon to one is a bane to another friend

We all play this "sane" game - and accept the risk as it comes

For some, it comes no more, that was their last one.

A pawn to them, a king to you, no distance too far to travel

Behind the board, behind the eyes, who really wins the prize?

To their surprise, it's not the ones who didn't survive.
