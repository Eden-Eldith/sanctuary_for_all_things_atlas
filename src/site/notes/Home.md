# Eden's Garden

**P.C. O'Brien | Eldith Cognitive Systems**

P.C. O'Brien (Eden Eldith) is an independent AI researcher and model developer who designs and trains language models from scratch on consumer hardware. His work spans neural-network architectures, multi-agent optimisation, computational physics, AI cognition and ethics.

This garden brings together the working systems, research papers, world reports and personal writing behind that work, from a garage in Gosport, UK.

## Models and Code

### WiggleGPT

A 124M-parameter transformer designed and trained from scratch on consumer hardware. Its learnable oscillating activation functions bring waveform-based neuron dynamics into language modelling.

[[Finalized work/WiggleGPT/WiggleGPT Paper\|Read the paper]] · [Code](https://github.com/Eden-Eldith/WiggleGPT) · [Model weights](https://huggingface.co/edeneldith/WiggleGPT) · [DOI](https://zenodo.org/records/17919011)

### COLM — Complex Oscillating Language Model

WiggleGPT's successor: a 498k-parameter complex-valued autoregressive language model. COLM promotes the oscillating activation from ℝ to ℂ, replaces self-attention with an O(N) complex-gated causal scan, and has no learned linear layers in its processing blocks.

[[Finalized work/COLM/COLM\|Read the paper]] · [Code](https://github.com/Eden-Eldith/COLM) · [Model weights](https://huggingface.co/edeneldith/COLM) · [Training corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/edeneldith/DCDM) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20118034)

**The story behind the models:** [[Finalized work/First_Sovereign_AI_Britain\|How I Built the First Sovereign AI in Britain]] — from a first line of Python to training WiggleGPT ten months later, and what sovereignty means when you build the model yourself.
